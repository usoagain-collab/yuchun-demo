# Pricing: Yu Chun signature naengmyeon

Inputs: 20 simulated buyers (`founder/panel/`), price curve (`founder/pricing-curve.md`), competitor prices (`founder/competitors.md`). **No cost data yet**, so margins are not calculated. Add food cost per dish to `founder/numbers.json` and run `/founder-cfo` before changing any price.

## 1. The price: keep $18.95

- Panel acceptable range $10.04 to $22.94. Median "getting expensive" is $20. $18.95 sits just under it.
- Competitors: So Gong Dong and Million both charge $18.95. Choi's Garden $22.40+. Gina's plate-lunch noodles $16.25. $18.95 is the market rate.
- Raw fish version at $21.95 is close to the ceiling ($22.94). Do not raise it.
- Do not raise the $18.95 bowl yet. The panel's problem is not the bowl price, it is the total bill.

## 2. The ladder

The top complaint: "noodles alone won't fill me, so I add a $21 to $39 main and it's $50+." Fix with a middle rung.

| rung | what | price | why step up |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Good | Naengmyeon bowl | $18.95 | the signature, as now |
| Better | **Naengmyeon + half kalbi or 4 mandoo** (new lunch combo) | $26.95 (test) | full meal for less than bowl + main ($39.90+). Matches Korea Garden's $26.99 kalbi + naengmyeon set |
| Best | Set A $65.95 / Set B $45.95 | as now | for 2+ people sharing BBQ |

## 3. Opening offer

Launch the combo as a **lunch combo, 11 AM to 3 PM**, for a set period (e.g. 8 weeks, ending a fixed date). Price it at the real price from day one. No "was $X" anchor.

## 4. What to test with real buyers

- Combo at **$24.95 vs $26.95**. Alternate weeks on the menu insert, count combo orders as share of noodle orders.
- Keep the bowl at $18.95 during the test so only one thing changes.

## 5. Top 3 objections (simulated buyers, not testimonials)

1. "$18.95 for a bowl that won't fill me up means adding mandoo or a stew, and with tax and tip I'm near $50 for one person." (P007)
2. "I already have my Korean place on Keeaumoku and I eat Yu Chun-style naengmyeon back home in Japan." (P001) → answer: say this is the original Honolulu shop.
3. "Kalbi at $38.95 plus the automatic 20% tip for five or more adds up quickly." (P013) → answer: a family set price for 5+.

## Limits

Simulated buyers lean agreeable; 65% buy rate is an upper bound. Competitor prices are mostly from undated Yelp menus; call to check before relying on them.
