## What would flip a no

- Hearing that this Honolulu shop is the original or serves something the Japanese branches don't, like a Hawaii-only dish.
- A lunch combo of the cold noodles with a small side of kalbi or a few mandoo for around $25 before tax.
- If my visiting family specifically asked for naengmyeon, or a friend I trust told me this place does it better than anywhere on Keeaumoku.
- A family set or BBQ combo for 4 to 6 people at a fixed price that comes out cheaper per head than ordering à la carte.
- Something only this Honolulu location has, like a Hawaii-only dish, that I can't get at the Japanese branches.
- A friend I trust telling me the chik naengmyeon is noticeably better or unlike anything else on Keeaumoku, or the raw fish version being something I can't get anywhere else nearby.
- A lunch special around $14 to $15 with a side, or a friend telling me the noodles are clearly better than anything I can get on the mainland.

Buyers say they would buy **1.2 times** in the first month on average.
