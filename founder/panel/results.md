## What would flip a no

- Seeing a strong Google or Yelp rating with lots of recent photos and reviews saying the naengmyeon is worth it, even for people who had never tried cold noodles.
- Seeing it recommended in a Japanese guidebook or by a Japanese blogger or Instagram account, with photos of the naengmyeon, especially on a very hot afternoon.
- Strong recent reviews or a local friend saying the kudzu naengmyeon is the best in town. Then I'd try one bowl.
- Knowing there's easy parking or validated parking and basically no wait at lunch.
- Seeing it recommended in a guidebook or a well-known travel blog as a must-try in Honolulu.
- A strong Google or Yelp rating, 4.5 or better with a lot of reviews, and photos showing a big bowl of noodles that really are made in house.
- Seeing it recommended on a Japanese travel blog or Instagram account, with photos of the noodles and easy directions from Waikiki by bus or a short taxi ride.
- A friend or someone in the Korean community I trust telling me their chik naengmyeon broth is the real thing, better than my usual place.
- A family or group set that feeds four for around $80 to $90, or a friend or relative telling me the naengmyeon is really the best in town.
- If I were already at Ala Moana Center shopping and saw it was a short walk with no wait, I'd probably stop in for lunch.
- A family or sharing set priced for four, around $100 for noodles plus a meat dish or two, so I know the total before we sit down.
- A lunch special around $14 for the naengmyeon, or a friend telling me it's really worth the money.

Buyers say they would buy **1.2 times** in the first month on average.
