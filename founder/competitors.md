# Yu Chun competitor price check (Honolulu Korean restaurants)

Researched 2026-11-21. Yu Chun reference prices (1159 Kapiolani Blvd): chik naengmyeon $18.95, kalbi $38.95, bulgogi $35.95, mandoo $20.95.

**How to read the dates.** Most restaurants here do not publish their own menu online, and DoorDash, Toast, Yelp business pages and menuweb blocked automated fetches. So most prices come from Yelp's menu pages, which carry no date and the label "Menu may not be up to date". For those, the "source date" column gives the date I retrieved the page (2026-11-21) and says it is undated. Treat every price as approximate until it is checked by phone or in person. Nothing below is estimated: anything I could not find is marked "not found".

## Where Yu Chun sits

1. **Naengmyeon at $18.95 matches the market.** It is the same as So Gong Dong (Keeaumoku) and Million (Kapiolani) for mul and bibim, below Choi's Garden ($22.40 to $23.34), and above plate-lunch noodles (Gina's $16.25). There is no room to undercut, and some room to go up toward Choi's.
2. **Kalbi at $38.95 sits between the two price tiers.** It is well above plate and casual kalbi ($23.40 to $27.95 at Gina's, So Gong Dong and the Million plate, and $26.99 for Korea Garden's kalbi + naengmyeon lunch set). It is below grilled kalbi platters ($47.95 at Million and Choi's). Bulgogi at $35.95 is also high next to So Gong Dong ($20.95) and Million ($32.95), and roughly level with Choi's ($38.93).
3. **Mandoo at $20.95 is in line with the market**: $17.95 to $22.40 at sit-down places, and $18.85 for a plate at Gina's. The clearest threat is a kalbi + naengmyeon lunch set like Korea Garden's ($26.99, with banchan and stew) or Million's ($36.95) against Yu Chun ordered à la carte.
