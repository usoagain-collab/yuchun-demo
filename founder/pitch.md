Yu Chun Korean Restaurant, 1159 Kapiolani Blvd, Honolulu (near Ala Moana Center, about 10 minutes from Waikiki). Open 11 AM to 9 PM every day.
Signature dish: black arrowroot (kudzu) naengmyeon, chewy noodles in an ice-cold tangy broth or tossed in a sweet-and-spicy sauce. $18.95 a bowl.
Also kalbi ($38.95), bulgogi ($35.95), mandoo, soups and pancakes ($20.95 to $29.95). Set B: 1 naengmyeon + 1 kalbi, bulgogi or spicy pork, $45.95.
Prices do not include tax. 20% gratuity is added for parties of 5 or more.
Walk in for 4 or fewer; reservations by phone for 5 or more. Cash and credit cards.
